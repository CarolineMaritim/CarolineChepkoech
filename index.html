01 / About

I'm a data and GIS professional with hands-on experience spanning geospatial analysis, environmental data management, project coordination, and fintech operations. Over the past several years, I've worked with large, messy, real-world datasets — validating them, mapping them, tracking them, and turning them into decisions that stakeholders could act on.

That work naturally pulled me toward the systems behind the data: how it's collected, cleaned, stored, and made reliable at scale. I'm now formalizing that direction through Codecademy's Data Engineer Career Path, building on my existing Python and SQL foundation with data pipelines, data modeling, and cloud fundamentals.

I care about data that can be trusted — accurate at the source, well-documented in transit, and clearly presented at the destination.
